# Face model notices

- `face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx`: YuNet model from OpenCV Zoo, licensed under MIT. Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet
- `face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx`: SFace model from OpenCV Zoo, licensed under Apache-2.0. Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_recognition_sface

The models and registered face images are used locally by Smart Search. The images are copied to `/home/jetauto/face_db` when `run_smart_search.bat` is run.
